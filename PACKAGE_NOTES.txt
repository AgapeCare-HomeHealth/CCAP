OCR DEPLOYMENT
- Plesk does not need Poppler, native Tesseract, Python, Docker, or server-level installation rights for OCR.
- The browser downloads pinned JavaScript/WASM engine assets from jsDelivr; `eng.traineddata.gz` is bundled under CCAP.Web/wwwroot/ocr/lang.
- The browser must be able to reach jsDelivr. If external CDNs are blocked by policy or CSP, vendor the runtime/core files locally before deployment.

PACKAGE HYGIENE

- Supply database/JWT/storage secrets through Plesk secure settings or an approved secret provider. Do not commit credentials or patient PDFs.
